Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Betall Chrome Extension ("we", "our", or "the Extension") handles information when you use our browser extension.

Summary: We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. The Extension only processes tweet text temporarily to find relevant prediction markets.

Information We Process

When you browse Twitter/X.com with the Extension enabled, we process:

  • Tweet Text: The text content of tweets displayed on your screen is sent to our server solely to find matching prediction markets on Polymarket.

How We Use This Information

Tweet text is used exclusively to:

  • Query our matching service to find relevant prediction markets
  • Display matching market information directly in your browser

This processing happens in real-time and no tweet content is stored on our servers after the matching process completes.

Information We Do NOT Collect

The Extension does not collect:

  • Your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • Your Twitter/X account information
  • Your browsing history
  • Your location data
  • Any cookies or tracking identifiers
  • Your Polymarket account information

Data Storage

The Extension stores only your preference settings (such as the similarity threshold for market matching) locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us
  • Can be cleared by uninstalling the Extension

Third-Party Services

The Extension interacts with:

  • Betall API: Our matching service that finds relevant prediction markets
  • Polymarket API: To retrieve market details and pricing information

When you click "Place Bet", you will be redirected to Polymarket's website, which has its own Privacy Policy.

Data Security

All communications between the Extension and our servers use HTTPS encryption. Since we don't store personal data, there is no personal data at risk.

Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
